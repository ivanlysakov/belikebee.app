Executive Brief: BeLikeBee.app
Tagline
Live in the city. Be like bee. Own a hive from anywhere.

Core Problem
City dwellers long for an authentic connection to nature, eco-friendly habits, and 100% pure, traceable honey, but lack the space, time, or knowledge to keep bees in urban environments. On the flip side, local beekeepers face high upfront operational costs, cash flow gaps early in the season, and limited distribution channels.

The Solution
BeLikeBee.app is a B2B2C remote farming platform and SaaS ecosystem connecting urban nature lovers with local beekeepers.

For Customers (Remote Beekeepers): Rent or adopt a dedicated hive in the countryside. Track its lifecycle in real time via a digital twin, order specific hive tasks, book live online workshops or hands-on apiary visits, and receive a guaranteed harvest of custom-branded, raw honey at the end of the season.

For Beekeepers (Apiary Management SaaS): A modern CRM to digitize apiary operations, secure upfront recurring revenue (subscriptions) before the harvest, and manage client bookings seamlessly.

Key Features & Mechanics
Digital Hive Feed: Real-time activity logs, photo/video updates, and health reports posted directly by the beekeeper.

Smart Booking & Scheduling: Flexible booking for live virtual inspections or in-person apiary masterclasses.

Subscription & Pre-Orders: Automated recurring payments powered by flexible local/global gateways (e.g., Monobank, Stripe).

Gamified Ownership: Customizable hive designs, personalized honey jars, and direct interaction with the beekeeper.

Target Audience
B2C: Urban professionals, eco-conscious families, and gift shoppers seeking interactive nature experiences.

B2B: Independent beekeepers looking to digitize their business and generate steady pre-season income.
